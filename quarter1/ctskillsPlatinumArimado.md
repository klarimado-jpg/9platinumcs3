## Computational Thinking Skills Activity

Date: August 13, 2025

Smart School Canteen

**4 Sub Problems:**

1. Students take too long to decide what to order. Some students are still choosing their food while already at the front of the line, which makes the queue move more slowly.

2. The cashier manually calculates the total and change. This can take extra time for every customer and may also lead to mistakes when there are many students ordering at once.

3. There is no system for monitoring food availability. Students may choose an item only to find out that it is already sold out, causing them to change their order and making the process even slower.

4. The canteen queue becomes too crowded during lunch break. Because ordering, payment, and checking food availability take time, more students continue to line up, resulting in longer waiting times.

**Applied CT Skills on Sub-Problem**

1. Subproblem: Students take too long to decide what to order
CT skill: Decomposition
Example solution: Create a digital menu that groups food items by category and shows the prices so that students can decide before reaching the cashier.
T
2. Subproblem: The cashier manually calculates the total and change
CT skill: Algorithm
Example Solution: Create a system that automatically adds the prices of selected items and calculates the correct change based on the amount paid.

3. Subproblem: There is no system for monitoring food availability
CT skill: Pattern Recognition
Solution: Track which food items are frequently sold and update their availability in the system when the stock becomes low.

4. Subproblem: The canteen queue becomes too crowded during lunch break
CT Skill: Decomposition
Solution: Separate the ordering and payment process into organized steps such as choosing food, confirming the order, paying, and receiving the food.

**Pseudocode for the identified problem**
There is no system for monitoring food availability

START
Display the menu with food items, prices, and availability.
Students choose food items.
IF the chosen food item is available THEN
    Add the item to the order.
ELSE
    Display "Sorry, this item is sold out."
    Ask the student to choose another item.
END
